Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about borrowing, returns, memberships, and using Apna Library — search below or browse by topic.

Membership
Click Register in the top navigation, fill in your name, email, and a password, and your account is created instantly. You can start browsing and reserving books right away.
Basic membership is free and gives you access to the full catalog. Premium membership unlocks extended loan periods and priority holds on popular titles.

Borrowing
Standard members can borrow up to 5 books at a time. Premium members can borrow up to 10.
Books are loaned for 14 days by default. You can renew a title once for an additional 7 days, as long as no one else has it on hold.

Returns & Fines
A small late fee accrues per day past the due date, capped at the replacement cost of the book. You'll see a reminder in your account 3 days before anything is due.
Contact us through the form below as soon as possible. We'll work out a replacement cost, and your account won't be restricted while we sort it out together.
